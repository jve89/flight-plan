# Energy levels

Johan prefers calm. Level 1 is the default; levels 2 and 3 are chosen in the intake when the
subject calls for it (a zoo can be busier and more exciting than a compliance tool).

| | 1. Calm (default) | 2. Lively | 3. Exuberant |
|---|---|---|---|
| Typical subjects | B2B, compliance, consultancy, professional services, SaaS | Consumer apps, events, hospitality, retail | Zoo, festival, kids, entertainment, games |
| Color | 1 accent + neutrals | 2 accents of similar lightness | Full palette from the subject, bold backgrounds per section allowed |
| Typeface | Inter only | Inter, maybe weight/width play | Display face for headlines may be proposed (ask), Inter for body |
| Whitespace | Generous | Generous | Tighter, more density allowed |
| Motion | One orchestrated moment per section, scroll-linked meaning | More scroll animation, hover life on interactive elements | Motion as a main character: parallax, playful transitions, characters |
| Imagery | Real footage, product UI, diagrams | Adds lifestyle photography, illustration | Illustration, 3D characters, stickers, collage |
| Shapes | Pills, soft radii | Same, plus occasional organic shapes | Free shapes, rotation, overlap |

Never changes, at any level (the pill header stays the default at every level too):
- Craft rules (tier 1): every visual is deliberate, never filler, mobile composition, accessibility, testing.
- Clear hierarchy: one primary CTA per section, readable type, contrast.
- Exuberant does not mean cluttered: it means more energy, still one idea per section.
