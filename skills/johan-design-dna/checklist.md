# Pre-delivery checklist

Run this after building and before delivering. Take real screenshots when the environment allows
(Playwright/Chromium): desktop 1440×900 and mobile 390×844, at the top and at several scroll
positions through every animated section. Fix, re-shoot, then deliver.

## DNA
- [ ] Inter loaded as webfont (or an agreed deviation).
- [ ] Pill header: floating, glass, one CTA; mobile menu works and is keyboard accessible.
- [ ] Hero layout follows the default (centered) or a requested/agreed choice, and looks current. Headline balanced (no dangling word), one or two sentences.
- [ ] One accent color at energy level 1; palette fits the subject.
- [ ] Deviations from tier 2 defaults are named in the plan or delivery note (requested by Johan, or proposed with a reason). None hidden.

## Craft
- [ ] Every visual is deliberate: it shows or explains something, or it is aesthetic by choice. No filler in slots that should explain.
- [ ] One memorable thing per section; no badge clouds.
- [ ] No template section stack; every section has one job.
- [ ] Infographics and 3D have their own mobile composition; smallest text on mobile ≥ 13px.
- [ ] Overlapping elements keep titles readable.
- [ ] Contrast: body 4.5:1, large text 3:1, text over video checked on the brightest frame.
- [ ] Visible focus states; real links and buttons; icon buttons have `aria-label`.
- [ ] `prefers-reduced-motion`: no pinning, final states shown, no autoplay video.
- [ ] Works without JS at least as readable static content.

## Motion
- [ ] Pinned scenes: captions in sync with phases; nothing jumps when the pin releases.
- [ ] No console errors; ScrollTrigger refreshed after fonts load.
- [ ] 3D renders only when visible; acceptable frame rate on a mid-range phone.

## Content
- [ ] No invented statistics, testimonials, logos or fake photos of work. Missing facts are clear
      placeholders like [UW PRIJS].
- [ ] Sentence case, plain verbs, CTA labels say what happens.
- [ ] Copy language matches the site's audience (Dutch, Estonian, English).

## Delivery note (2–4 lines)
- Which archetype, theme and energy level were used.
- Which deviations were requested or agreed.
- What was fixed during review.
