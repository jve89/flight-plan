From plan to permission in five steps.

You describe what you want to do. We turn it into the file an authority can approve.

  1. Step 1

    Describe the operation

    What, where, how high and with which drone. This concept of operations is the base of every application.

  2. Step 2

    Assess the ground risk

    How many people could be below the flight, and how serious an impact could be.

  3. Step 3

    Assess the air risk

    Which airspace you fly in, and how likely you are to meet manned aircraft.

  4. Step 4

    Set the safety level

    Ground and air risk combine into a SAIL. The SAIL decides how robust your safety measures must be.

  5. Step 5

    Submit to the authority

    We submit the file and handle the questions until the authorisation is issued.

Every flight has a defined volume.

An authority does not only look at your route. It looks at the space around it, and at what happens if something goes wrong.

  • Flight geography: where you plan to fly
  • Contingency volume: room to recover
  • Ground risk buffer: protects people outside

First the route you want to fly. Around it, the volumes the SORA asks you to define. The bigger the buffer, the fewer people can be affected.
